:::{glossary}
ABSR
: Auto-Balanced Structural Reflection. The method that expands a country's coarser IO table onto the model's common sector schema, using U.S. structure as weights, while keeping the country's accounts balanced.

Basic price
: The amount a producer receives for a product, excluding taxes on products and including subsidies on products.

BACI
: The CEPII database of bilateral international trade by product, used to split each country's imports by country of origin.

Country tier
: A group of countries modeled with the same level of detail and the same kinds of data sources, from Tier 0 (United States) to Tier 3 (template-based).

EEIO
: Environmentally-extended input-output model. An input-output model of an economy with environmental data (here, greenhouse gas emissions) attached to each sector.

FIGARO
: Full International and Global Accounts for Research in input-Output analysis. Multi-regional IO tables from Eurostat and the European Commission's Joint Research Centre, used for many Tier 2 countries.

GWP
: Global warming potential. The warming effect of 1 kg of a gas relative to 1 kg of CO₂ over a time horizon. The model uses AR6 values with a 100-year horizon.

Make/Use framework
: IO tables that record which industries make which commodities (Make, or supply) and which commodities each industry and final user buys (Use).

Margins
: The trade and transport costs added between the producer and the buyer: transport, wholesale, and retail.

MRIO
: Multi-regional input-output model. Country IO tables linked into one global system through international trade.

OECD ICIO
: The OECD Inter-Country Input-Output tables, used for Tier 2 countries not covered by FIGARO.

Producer price
: The basic price plus taxes on products, less subsidies on products, excluding margins.

Purchaser price
: The price the buyer pays: the producer price plus trade and transport margins. The published factors are per dollar of purchaser price.

RAS
: An iterative method that scales the rows and columns of a table until it matches known row and column totals.

Redefinitions
: Adjustments that move secondary products (and their inputs) to the industry where they are the primary product.

Structural reflection
: Using a detailed table's structure (here, the U.S. tables) as weights to split a coarser table into finer sectors.
:::
