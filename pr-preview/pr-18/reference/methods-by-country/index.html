Methods by country

Summary

The IO table and greenhouse gas data behind each of the 148 countries in the model, with the year of each.

Draft

Planned for v1.0: a filterable table with a clickable map, and per-country details on trade reconciliation, accounting basis, currency and price index, and benchmarked sectors.

Each country’s tier sets which kind of IO table and emissions data it starts from. See Country tiers for the IO tables and GHG data by country tier for the emissions data.

All IO tables are converted to 2024 U.S. dollars. Emissions stay at their data year, so a country’s direct emission intensities are its data-year emissions per 2024 dollar of output. Tier 3 countries are the exception: their intensities are 2018 emissions per 2018 dollar (see Data years).

The rest-of-world region is not in the table. Its input recipe and emission intensities are pooled from Colombia, Mexico, Türkiye, and Chile.
