What's in a release

Summary

The Cornerstone EEIO model is updated annually and versioned with the year of the release, so Cornerstone EEIO 2026 is released in 2026 and the 2027 version will be released in 2027. Every year, the releases will be publicly available in October. Each release publishes emission factors covering 405 sectors across 148 countries, 21 regions and one global average. To download the latest version, check out Zenodo.

The release¶

The release includes one Excel workbook that contains: a cover, a data dictionary, a producer-price tab and a purchaser-price tab for each year the data is available for, and five auxiliary tabs.

Cover¶

The first tab introduces the dataset and the release details:

  • Description: cradle-to-gate greenhouse gas emission factors in kg CO₂e per USD, by good or service, location and year, in producer and purchaser price. Prices are in current-year dollars, so the dollar year matches the data year.

  • Coverage: 148 countries and 21 regions, and 405 categories of goods and services (government services are not covered). The total and each gas (CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, HFCs, PFCs, NF₃, SF₆) are given in CO₂e, annually from 2018 to 2026.

  • Publisher and license: Cornerstone Sustainability Data Initiative, under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.

Below these details, the cover lists every other tab with a one-line description and points readers to the data dictionary and the model documentation.

Producer and purchaser price tabs¶

There are two tabs for each year, with identical layouts: {YEAR}PRO for producer prices and {YEAR}PUR for purchaser prices. The producer price is the cost to the producer plus their profits and taxes paid. The purchaser price adds the value of sales and distribution. Read more about how to choose between purchaser and producer prices in How-to 2: Purchaser vs. producer price, and margins.

Each row is one sector in one location, and the columns are:

ColumnMeaning
CodeGood or service code, based on the BEA input-output commodity codes, which derive from NAICS. There are 405 categories.
NameName of the good or service.
LocationA UN three-letter country code, with additions for multi-country regions.
Total GHGsSum of all greenhouse gases, in kg CO₂e per USD.
CO2Carbon dioxide, in kg CO₂e per USD.
CH4Methane, in kg CO₂e per USD.
N2ONitrous oxide, in kg CO₂e per USD.
HFCsHydrofluorocarbons, in kg CO₂e per USD.
PFCsPerfluorocarbons, in kg CO₂e per USD.
NF3Nitrogen trifluoride, in kg CO₂e per USD.
SF6Sulfur hexafluoride, in kg CO₂e per USD.

Values are in current-year dollars: a factor in a 2024 tab applies to spend in 2024 dollars.

Auxiliary tabs¶

The data dictionary and five other tabs support the data tabs. The data dictionary is the second tab in the workbook; the others follow the price tabs.

TabWhat it contains
Data DictionaryDefinitions of each field in the data tabs.
Exchange ratesLocal currency units per US dollar for 148 countries: country code and name, currency, unit, and one column per year from 2018 to 2026. Rates come from Open Exchange Rates (openexchangerates.org) and are Cornerstone’s recommended rates for converting spend in local currency before applying the factors.
GWPThe 100-year global warming potentials from the IPCC AR6 that express each gas in CO₂e: CO₂ 1, CH₄ 27.9 (to confirm), N₂O 273, SF₆ 24,300 and NF₃ 17,400. HFCs and PFCs are already in CO₂e, so their factor is 1.
Cornerstone <> NAICS mappingThe correspondence between NAICS 2022 codes and Cornerstone codes: NAICS code and name, Cornerstone code and title, and a weight. When a NAICS industry is split across several Cornerstone codes, the weights show each share and add up to 1.
GoodsServicesThe 405 sectors with their code, name and description. Cornerstone recommends using this description to map spend categories to emission factors.
LocationsEvery location code and name used in the model: countries by their three-letter code, and regions.

Update cadence¶

The Cornerstone EEIO follows an annual update cycle. Every year we release a version named after the release year, so Cornerstone 2027 will be released in 2027. Releases are publicly available every October so companies can plan accordingly.
