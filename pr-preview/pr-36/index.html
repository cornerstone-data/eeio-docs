Cornerstone EEIO

The Cornerstone EEIO model is a multi-regional environmentally-extended input-output model. It links countries’ economies through international trade and attaches greenhouse gas emissions to every sector. Cornerstone EEIO is the successor of the USEEIO and CEDA models; see Origins of the Cornerstone EEIO.

Cornerstone EEIO 2026 was developed to produce cradle-to-gate emission factors for the goods and services that organizations commonly buy. These emission factors are the primary data product of the 2026 release: spend-based factors, in kg CO₂e per USD, for 405 sectors across 148 countries, 21 regional averages and one global average. They are most commonly used for Scope 3 measurements in corporate inventories and for academic and policy research.

The 2026-rc release publishes the spend-based GHG emission factors. The full 2026 release adds a 2017–2026 time series and will be accompanied by the model matrices, in a format still to be decided.
