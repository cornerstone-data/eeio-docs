Methods by country

Summary

The IO table and greenhouse gas data behind each of the 148 countries in the model, with the data year of each.

Draft

Planned for v1.0: a filterable table with a clickable map, and per-country details on trade reconciliation, accounting basis, currency and price index, and benchmarked sectors.

Each country’s tier sets which kind of IO table and emissions data it starts from. See IO sources by country tier for the IO tables and GHG data by country tier for the emissions data.

The data sources and years used can differ between the dollar years that factors are published for, so the table is given for each set of dollar years that share the same data.

Tier 3 countries share one IO template, a blend of Malaysia (30%) and Tunisia (40%) from the ICIO for 2020 and Mexico (30%) from FIGARO for 2023; the table gives 2020, the year of most of the blend. They also share one GHG template, scaled to each country’s fossil CO₂ total from EDGAR (see Tier 3: Template emissions).

The rest-of-world region is not in the table. Its input recipe and emission intensities are pooled from Colombia, Mexico, Türkiye, and Chile.
