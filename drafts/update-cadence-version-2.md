The Cornerstone EEIO follows a version lifecycle, similar to the long-term support model used in software. About every two years, we release a new version with a large methodology update, named after its release year (for example, the 2026 methods and the 2028 methods).

Each version is supported for about five years. For at least three years, it receives annual updates every October with data updates and bug fixes, and its methods do not change. It then moves into extended support for two more years.

Companies can adopt a version when they are ready and know they will have about five years of support to set targets and measure progress, while new versions keep improving the model.
