:::{tip} Summary
Each Cornerstone EEIO release is a version of the model together with the data products published from it. For Cornerstone EEIO 2026, the primary data product is the set of spend-based GHG emission factors. This page lists each release, the data products it publishes, and its release note and change log. Download the data from [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/doi/10.5281/zenodo.23396891).
:::

## The model and its data products

The **Cornerstone EEIO model** is the multi-regional input-output model described in [the IO model](../io/index.md) and [the GHG extension](../ee/index.md). A **data product** is something published from the model for others to use. Cornerstone EEIO 2026 was developed to produce cradle-to-gate emission factors for the goods and services that organizations commonly buy, so the emission factors are its primary data product. How they are calculated from the model is described in [](../factors/calculation.md).

| Release | Status | Data products |
|---|---|---|
| [2026-rc](rc.md) | Release candidate, October 27, 2026 | Spend-based GHG emission factors for 2024, 2025 and 2026, at producer and purchaser price (see [](../factors/whats-in-a-release.md)) |
| 2026 | Planned, December 2026 | Spend-based GHG emission factors for 2017 to 2026, accompanied by the model matrices (format to be decided) |
